About Our Heritage Plywood Sandakelum

The business Sandakelum started with the supply of quality timber planks and has steadily grown into one of Ganemulla's leading specialized suppliers of engineered plywood boards and composite sheets. Having earned the trust of carpenters, contractors, and furniture makers for over twenty years, we are dedicated to uncompromised quality and dependable service.

We stock an extensive inventory of certified imported and local plywood varieties in Sri Lanka. Contact us for bulk quotations, custom cuts, and expert guidance on sheet specifications.
